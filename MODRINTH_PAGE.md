```markdown
# Ten Pin Bowling

Ten-pin bowling for Minecraft, with physics derived from the real sport rather
than invented.

**Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge · MIT · no dependencies**

> **Beta.** Playable and tuned, but the constants are still moving. Every one of
> them can be changed in game — see *Tuning* below. If you disagree with a
> number, change it and tell me.

## Playing

Build a lane, place the foul line, right-click with the ball.

- **Aim** with where you stand and where you look
- **Hold** right-click for power
- **Arrow keys** for spin — this is the hook, and it is the whole game
- The lane keeps frame state and the scoreboard, so a game survives logging out,
  and several players can share one lane

The ball returns on its own. You only ever need the one.

## What is actually simulated

- Ball 7.26 kg, pin 1.53 kg. Restitution about 0.5 ball-on-pin and 0.7
  pin-on-pin, applied along the line of centres — a pin clipped on its left edge
  goes right, not straight on
- Release at 19.7 mph, inside the real 17–21 range
- A **40-foot oil pattern on a 60-foot lane**, so the ball skids for two thirds
  and only bites in the last third. That late bite is why a hook looks straight
  and then turns hard
- Fallen pins **sweep their body across the deck** instead of sliding about as
  upright cylinders. Six of the ten in a strike come from pin action, not from
  the ball
- **Kickback walls** on the pin deck, because the 6 rebounding off the right
  wall is how the 10 pin actually falls
- **Release variation**, because a human hand does not repeat a delivery exactly

Measured over 40 throws at the pocket: **22% strikes, mean 8.45 pins**, mode of
9, most common leave a single 6 or 10. That is club-bowler bowling, not
professional bowling.

A dead straight ball into the head pin is the *weak* shot, exactly as in real
life — it pays the full deflection toll and leaves a split. The pocket sits a
degree or so off centre, with hook on it.

## Tuning

Every physics constant is settable at runtime through `/bowlsim`, which is
**permission level 2 (ops)** because it retunes live physics for everyone on the
server. A single-player host has it by default.

## Assets

All textures are generated by `tools/gen_textures.py` in the source repository —
each image drawn from geometry and a named palette, nothing traced, sampled or
copied. Run the script and the entire asset set rebuilds identically.

## Disclosure

This project was written with AI assistance, disclosed under Modrinth's rules.
The design, the physics research, the in-game measurement and tuning, and the
bug-hunting are the author's; an assistant wrote code to that direction.

Source: https://github.com/max-champlin/bowling (MIT)
```

## 4. Also fixed in the jar

`neoforge.mods.toml` declared `versionRange = "[1.21.1,1.22)"`, so Modrinth
listed the mod as supporting **1.21.1 through 1.21.11** while the page said
1.21.1 only. That mismatch is the most defensible reading of the "Description
Clarity" complaint, and it was genuinely wrong: NeoForge 21.2+ changes the
entity and rendering APIs this mod lives in, so the untested versions would have
broken.
